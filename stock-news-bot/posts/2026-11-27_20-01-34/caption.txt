Zee Entertainment's Rs600 crore share buyback will run from December 1 to 7 at Rs91 per share. Eligible shareholders of record on November 25 may tender about one share for every 14 held. Promoters will not participate; bid settlement is scheduled by December 14. This is an important development for investors tracking the Indian stock market. Keep a close eye on how this story unfolds over the next few trading sessions, and think about what it could mean for your portfolio and investment strategy.
