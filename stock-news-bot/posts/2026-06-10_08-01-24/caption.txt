Fertiliser stocks like FACT and Chambal Fertilisers rose by up to 5% as reports indicated a potential increase in government subsidies for FY27. The Ministry of Fertilisers has proposed doubling the subsidy allocation to Rs1.71 lakh crore amid rising global fertiliser prices.
