The government has reduced the sugar stockholding limit for dealers to 2,000 quintals from 4,000 to curb hoarding and maintain stable prices. This change, effective September 15, follows earlier measures to enhance sugar availability and protect the market ahead of increased imports. This is an important development for investors tracking the Indian stock market.
