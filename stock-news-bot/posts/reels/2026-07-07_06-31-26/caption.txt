Bajaj Auto's Rs5,633 crore share buyback ends today, offering shareholders Rs12,000 per share, 20% above the last closing. The buyback includes 46.94 lakh shares and targets 1.68% of capital. Eligible shareholders can tender shares through stockbrokers until July 10, 2026. This is an important development for investors tracking the Indian stock market. Keep a close eye on how this story unfolds over the next few trading sessions, and think about what it could mean for your portfolio and investment strategy.
