Tata Steel plans to buy a 26.38% stake in APL Apollo Tubes for about Rs15,511 crore and make an open offer for another 26%. It will also transfer its tubes and pipes business and sell Tata Steel Colors to APL Apollo in deals worth up to Rs28,000 crore combined. This is an important development for investors tracking the Indian stock market.
