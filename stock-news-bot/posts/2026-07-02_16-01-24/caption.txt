Coal India reported a 5.9% year-on-year increase in coal supplies to power plants, reaching 51.44 MT in June. The rise is attributed to peak summer electricity demand. Overall coal supplies rose 7.5%, with significant growth in the non-regulated sector, enhancing supply chain efficiency.
